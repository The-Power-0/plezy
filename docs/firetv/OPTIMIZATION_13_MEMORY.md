Stage 13 — Fire TV memory policy. On reduced-tier Fire Sticks the in-memory Flutter artwork cache is limited to 300 entries / 48 MiB rather than 400 / 64 MiB. Other Fire TV devices are limited to 400 entries and up to 96 MiB, compared with 500 entries and up to 128 MiB on true 4K displays. Disk artwork caching, decode-resolution policy, Skia GPU budget, Media3 decoder allocations and normal Plezy build budgets are untouched. This is a memory/headroom vs cache-hit tradeoff; PSS/GC/texture eviction and visible-artwork tests are required before production.
